### Custom Orange Logic integrations
Some jobs are too specific for the standard product. We build them anyway. Recent integrations:

- [Folder-path metadata extraction](/blog/2026-folder-paths-to-records/), for File On Demand: one clean record from many copies.
- [Generative-AI provenance](/blog/2026-dam-integrations/): where an AI-generated asset came from.
- [Invisible watermarking](/blog/2026-dam-integrations/): marks that do not change how an asset looks.
- [Custom image classification](/blog/2026-dam-integrations/), with LandingAI LandingLens, trained on your own assets.
- Salesforce Marketing Cloud: an integration with the platform.

### AI and agent integration
We build Reckons.AI so a person reviews what the AI proposes. Matthew Roe pioneered Orange Logic Agents, including the OpenAI suggested-metadata integration, now a public feature. We use LLMs and other AI tools heavily in Reckons.AI, the custom software behind this site.

AI and agentic automations and integrations are deliverables within our consulting work, not a separate product.

### Custom software — Reckons.AI
@tag: alpha
Reckons.AI is the project behind this site: MIT licensed, works offline, no account, no seat count, no usage meter, and your data in plain-text files you can read.

Honest status: alpha. A small RDFS/OWL subset, no SPARQL engine yet.

**Why it matters.** A post arguing that this kind of stack should not cost six figures a year reached about 212,000 impressions — a sign of interest in the problem, not a count of Reckons.AI users.

**Coming.** Demos of asset generation and other local, subscription-free processes grounded in human-controlled details.
