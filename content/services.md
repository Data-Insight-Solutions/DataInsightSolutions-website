## Enterprise Orange Logic consulting

Digital asset management (DAM) is the system a company uses to store, organize, and deliver its files, images, and media. My consulting work centers on Orange Logic.

I was Solution Engineer (November 2023 – August 2024), then Solutions Architect (August 2024 – September 2025), at Orange Logic, serving many clients on automation and integration projects. Most of that work can't be named. Right now I'm doing the same kind of work at enterprise scale: Orange Logic automations and integrations at VF Corp.

## How we work together

We start with a discovery: a free, multi-hour consultation for companies with over $1M in revenue, or a 30-minute discovery for $50. If the fit is right for both of us, I write a statement of work. The work is project-based, at a minimum of $100 per hour, with discounts available for local and nonprofit organizations.

## Custom Orange Logic integrations

Some jobs are too specific for the standard product. I build them anyway. Recent integrations:

- **Metadata extraction from File On Demand virtual folder paths.** Finds binary versions and metadata for the same filename across locations and consolidates them. The result: fewer duplicate records than traditional file storage like SharePoint or Google Drive, better search, and record relationships built from the extracted metadata.
- **Generative-AI provenance.** Tracking where AI-generated assets come from.
- **Invisible watermarking.** Marking digital assets in a way that doesn't change how they look.
- **Custom image classification.** Computer-vision models trained on your own assets, built as LandingAI LandingLens projects.
- **Salesforce Marketing Cloud.** A Salesforce Marketing Cloud integration.

## AI and agent integration

I build Reckons.AI so a person reviews what the AI proposes. I pioneered Orange Logic Agents, including the OpenAI suggested-metadata integration, now a public feature. I use LLMs and other AI tools heavily in Reckons.AI, the custom software behind this site.

AI and agentic automations and integrations are deliverables within my consulting work, not a separate product.

## Custom software — Reckons.AI

Reckons.AI is the project behind this site: MIT licensed, works offline, no account, no seat count, no usage meter, and your data in plain-text files you can read.

Honest status: alpha. A small RDFS/OWL subset, no SPARQL engine yet, one maintainer.

**Why it matters.** A post arguing that this kind of stack should not cost six figures a year reached about 212,000 impressions — a sign of interest in the problem, not a count of Reckons.AI users.

**Coming.** Demos of asset generation and other local, subscription-free processes grounded in human-controlled details.
